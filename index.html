Who, What, Where is God?

Friday, 11 September 2026 6:30 PM – Sunday, 13 September 2026 12:00 PM
Wellington Meeting House
“Seeing Earth from space made me feel connected to all living things on our planet.” Mike Massimino, Astronaut
Facilitated by Murray Short & Janet McKean

Commencing with dinner on Friday at 6.30pm and ending with Sunday Meeting for Worship

How we think about the spiritual dimension of human experience matters. As Ilia Delio has suggested, 'The way we understand God, ourselves, and our relationship to the natural world shapes every aspect of how we live and act.'

Join us in exploring different ways of thinking about spirituality and religion and how they can shape the way we live and act. Presenters will share their perspectives, from supernatural theism to panentheism and non-theism, and each will provide opportunities for group discussion.

The presenters will be Adrian Skelton, Janet McKean, Mark Crozier, Murray Short and Trish McBride.

Options of online or in-person participation are available. Those who are joining online are encouraged to do so in the company of others in their worship group so they can have in-person sharing and discussion.

In-person participants need to make their own arrangements and payment for accommodation. Billets and two other options are identified on the registration form. Numbers are limited and there are motels nearby.

Send any queries to Murray at mandns@xtra.co.nz
